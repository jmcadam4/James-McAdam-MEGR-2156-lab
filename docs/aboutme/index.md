#About Me

###James McAdam

I am a junior year mechanical engineering student as of fall 2026 and have enjoyed the pursuit of understanding mechanical systems and their designs for many years before I came to UNCC. I worked on bikes and cars a lot in middle school then found my way to robotics in high school. I knew for a long time that I wanted to be a mechanical engineer however the experience of working with a team on a tight schedule to get a working robot built and deployed really solidified that belief. I find a lot of satisfaction and purpose in solving problems, the pursuit of tackling bigger and bigger ones is incredibly rewarding. Every problem I have approached, I have used something I learned from a previous project. I've been able to bridge gaps between seemingly unrelated problems through technical skills, physics, manufacturing considerations, and many other things. This made it apparent to me that becoming a good engineer wouldn't just require me to crunch numbers, it would also require me to get a thorough grasp of the mechanics behind everything around us. This realization helped me feel excited about my degree again during the time at which it is almost entirely crunching numbers for an answer and not as a part of solving a bigger problem. I got curious again and I think that will make me a much better and happier engineer. I also find organization and cooperation amongst different subsets of engineers to get one product built quite interesting. A cohesive team can get a project done with a better result in a fraction of the time, and seeing it in practice made me start thinking about the logistics of getting a problem solved just as much as how to solve it. I don't see myself becoming an engineering manager where that intuition becomes really important but I still think it can help the team perform when everyone is thinking about things like that. These two practices, not alone but largely, make me excited to become a professional mechanical engineer soon.
